04 / About

An engineer's mindset, with a builder's bias.

I’m an engineering-focused project builder interested in how systems behave, where constraints emerge and how data can support a better decision.

My work brings together industrial engineering methods and practical software development: modelling queues and inventory, optimizing production and distribution, exploring disruptions, and turning analysis into outputs people can understand.

I value clear assumptions, measurable outcomes and honest reporting. I’m keen to keep learning through internships, collaborative projects and opportunities where analytical thinking can help solve real operational problems.
